Academia: teaching and psycholinguistic research

Full-time lecturer on the tenure track at Homs University, where my work combines teaching, research and curriculum development in English language and linguistics.

Lecturer (tenure-track), Faculty of Humanities

Homs University, Syria. I teach English grammar to undergraduate English Literature students. Earlier courses have included academic writing, English phonetics and translation.

My doctoral research examined memory and parsing strategies in multilingual sentence comprehension.

Research interests

  • Sentence comprehension
  • Working memory and language processing
  • Parsing and syntax
  • Multilingualism
  • L1 transfer
  • Explicit knowledge and production
  • Automaticity
