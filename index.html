About

A career built around language and communication.

I am a linguist and university lecturer whose work focuses on language comprehension, multilingualism and the relationship between language and the mind. I hold a PhD in Linguistics from Jawaharlal Nehru University, where my research examined memory and parsing strategies in multilingual sentence comprehension.

Alongside academia, I work as a certified Arabic-English conference interpreter and as an international journalist and field producer. These roles have taken me into classrooms, international conferences, diplomatic settings and field reporting environments.

This website brings those strands together in one place: a resource for my students and a professional portfolio for institutions, conference organizers, media organizations and clients.
